Background

I am passionate about many things, but one of the things I enjoy the most is learning, especially by working on real projects. I started developing this mindset at university while working on engineering projects, and I continue to apply it today through personal projects and independent research.

Most of my projects are inspired by things I encounter in everyday life. I saw a competition robot and wanted to build one myself. I watched someone create a bipedal robot on YouTube and wondered if I could do the same. After buying a smart switch, I started thinking about how adding a display could improve its functionality and user experience (and it also looks cool).

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Electronics Engineering and am eager to broaden my horizons through new experiences and challenges. My approach combines strong technical foundations, thorough documentation review, and careful design analysis with the practical implementation of effective solutions.
